drm/fl2000: Add KMS display pipeline

Register the DRM device once all FL2000 interfaces are bound and the
IT66121 component is available, and connect the pieces:

- a primary plane, a cursor plane, a CRTC and an encoder, with GEM
  SHMEM framebuffers accessed through the shadow-plane helpers and
  XRGB8888 as the only format. The framebuffer is only read by the CPU,
  so it does not need to be physically contiguous. The primary plane
  passes its merged damage to the stream; the cursor plane hands over
  its sprite, or only its position when the framebuffer is unchanged.
  Without a cursor plane, compositors keep the pointer on the primary
  GPU and it never appears on this output
- mode validation and PLL search in the CRTC's atomic_check (which may
  adjust htotal and the clock in adjusted_mode), and FL2000 programming
  in mode_set_nofb, before the bridge's mode_set
- vblank from the DRM vblank timer, as the FL2000 has no vblank
  interrupt, and fbdev emulation
- the FL2000 interrupt endpoint, which reports monitor and IT66121
  events and triggers a hotplug event

Tested on x86_64 with a UGREEN USB 3.0 to HDMI adapter (FL2000DX +
IT66121FN) and a 1920x1080 monitor: EDID, mode setting and a GNOME
Wayland desktop work, with the cursor, at 59 frames per second on the
link and no measurable effect on the rest of the system.
