drm/fl2000: Add frame streaming

Frames are sent as one bulk transfer per frame on endpoint 1 OUT, after
switching the AV control interface to altsetting 1. A small pool of
scatter-gather buffers (enough for three frames in flight plus one being
filled) cycles between a render list, a transmit list and the in-flight
list. When no new frame is ready, the previous one is sent again so the
FL2000 always has data to scan out.

Frames are converted with the drm_fb_xrgb8888_to_rgb888()/_rgb565()
helpers; the FL2000 additionally wants the two 32-bit halves of every
64-bit word swapped, which is done over the whole buffer. Conversion and
frame copies run with the buffer taken off the lists so that list_lock,
which is also taken from URB completion, is never held across
multi-megabyte work.

The stream is wired up to KMS in the next patch.
