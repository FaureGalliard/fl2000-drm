drm: Add Fresco Logic FL2000 USB display driver

Hi,

This series adds a DRM driver for USB display adapters built around the
Fresco Logic FL2000DX (USB ID 1d5c:2000). These are sold under many
brands as cheap USB 3.0 to VGA/HDMI dongles; the HDMI variants pair the
FL2000 with an ITE IT66121 HDMI transmitter on the FL2000's I2C bus.
Today these adapters only work on Linux with out-of-tree drivers.

The driver started as Artem Mygaiev's out-of-tree fl2000_drm driver.
I ported it to current kernels, fixed the problems found on real
hardware (EDID, bridge lifetime across USB resets, crashes on
disconnect) and cleaned it up for submission.

The series builds the driver up in four steps, each of which compiles
on its own:

  1. USB driver, FL2000 register access (regmap) and I2C adapter
  2. IT66121 HDMI transmitter bridge, including the EDID fallback
  3. frame streaming over bulk URBs
  4. the KMS pipeline, which makes the display usable

It is posted as an RFC because there are design points I would like
feedback on before doing the larger reworks:

1. IT66121 support. The series carries its own small IT66121 bridge
   (fl2000_it66121.c) instead of reusing drivers/gpu/drm/bridge/
   ite-it66121.c. The in-tree driver is probed from device tree and
   reads EDID through the IT66121 DDC engine, but on the tested adapter
   the monitor's DDC lines are wired to the FL2000 I2C bus and the
   IT66121 DDC engine never completes an EDID read. What would be the
   preferred direction: instantiate ite-it66121 from the USB driver
   (software node) and let it attach with DRM_BRIDGE_ATTACH_NO_CONNECTOR
   while the FL2000 side provides the DDC adapter to a bridge connector,
   or keep the IT66121 handling private to this driver?

2. The bridge still creates its own connector. Converting it to
   DRM_BRIDGE_ATTACH_NO_CONNECTOR and drm_bridge_connector is planned
   for v2, but depends on the answer to (1).

3. The FL2000 exposes three USB interfaces, and the state they share
   (regmap, I2C adapter, IT66121 client, component match) is kept as
   drvdata and devres of the struct usb_device rather than of an
   interface, so that it survives the unbind/rebind cycles of
   usb_reset_device(). Is there a better established pattern for a
   multi-interface device that needs this?

The rest of the pipeline follows the current helpers: regular atomic
plane/CRTC/encoder objects, GEM SHMEM with shadow-plane access, the
drm_fb_xrgb8888_to_*() conversion helpers, the DRM vblank timer (the
FL2000 has no vblank interrupt) and fbdev emulation through
drm_client_setup().

Other known limitations: no suspend/resume handling, damage clips only
decide whether to update (every update converts the whole frame, since
the stream rotates through several buffers), the USB bandwidth check
that should switch to RGB565 on slow links is not functional yet
(RGB888 is always used), and VGA-only FL2000 adapters are untested.

Tested on x86_64 (amdgpu laptop, GNOME Wayland) with a UGREEN USB 3.0
to HDMI adapter and a 1920x1080 monitor: EDID, mode setting and the
desktop work. Every patch builds without warnings with W=1 on top of
the current mainline development tree, and the series passes
checkpatch --strict.
